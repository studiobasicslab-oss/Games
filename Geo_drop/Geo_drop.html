How to Play Geo-Drop

1. Target Location Flashes: A city, landmark, or natural wonder appears at the top of the screen.

2. Rapid Timer Ticks Down: You have 3 seconds or 5 seconds to locate it on the real-scale world map.

3. Drop Your Pin: Click or tap on the map as close as possible to the target.

4. 2-Second On-Map Reveal: The map flies to both pins and opens an interactive popup showing the real target location, exact country name, and distance off in kilometers & miles for 2 seconds before presenting the round summary card.

5. Expert Mode: Try "Location Name Only" mode where country hints are stripped completely!
