📖 CRYPTANALYST'S FIELD MANUAL

Cipher mechanics, frequency curves & historical decryption methods

1. Letter Frequency Analysis (Al-Kindi / 9th Century)

In natural English text, letters do not appear with equal probability. 'E' is the most frequent (~12.7%), followed by 'T', 'A', 'O', 'I', 'N'. In monoalphabetic substitution ciphers, the highest frequency ciphertext letter almost always corresponds to E or T.

2. Polyalphabetic Vigenère Ciphers

Uses a repeating keyword to shift each letter by a different amount: $C_i = (P_i + K_{i \pmod L}) \pmod{26}$. Broken using the Kasiski examination to find repeating distance multiples, revealing keyword length $L$.

3. The Enigma Machine

An electro-mechanical rotor cipher machine. Each keystroke advances the rightmost rotor by $1/26$th of a turn, constantly mutating the circuit pathway. Cracked by Marian Rejewski and Alan Turing's electromechanical 'Bombe' machines.
