📖 How to Play Lexicon Dash

1. The Goal: Submit valid words starting with the specified letter for each category slot before time runs out.

2. 4-Tier Categories: Foundational ($1.0\times$), Pop Culture ($1.25\times$), STEM & Geography ($1.5\times$), and Niche/Expert ($2.0\times$).

3. 3-Layer Validation: Exact Set match ($100\%$), Levenshtein edit distance ($90\%$), and Live Fallback enrichment.

4. Rarity Multiplier: Rare and obscure words award up to $+96\%$ bonus points and Star Dust!

5. Arena Slam Buzzer: Be the first to fill all slots and slam the buzzer to inflict a 15-second panic clock on your opponents.
