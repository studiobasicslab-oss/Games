🎮 How to Play Sequence Conductor

Sequence Conductor challenges your brain's working memory by requiring you to process sequence patterns under active transformation rules.

1. Direct Echo: Click the exact pads in the order they light up.
2. Reverse Recall: Enter the nodes in backward order (last node first).
3. Spatial Mirror: Enter the diagonally opposite node for each item.
4. Numeric Shift (+1): Shift each pad value by +1 clockwise around the board.
5. Conductor's Blitz: Rules change dynamically on every round!

Tip: Sound tones are pitched in harmonic intervals to assist auditory memory encoding.
