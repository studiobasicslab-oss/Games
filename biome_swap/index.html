🌍 Keystone Ecology Codex & Rules

1. Trophic Cascades: Keystone species trigger powerful top-down chain reactions. For example, adding wolves stops deer overgrazing, letting willow trees regrow, which brings back beavers and creates wetlands!

2. Lindeman's 10% Rule: Energy decreases by ~90% at each step up the food pyramid. You cannot sustain apex predators without a massive foundation of producers and herbivores.

3. Invasive Species: Toxic invaders (cane toads, lionfish, kudzu) spread aggressively unless combated with targeted Biocontrol agents and controlled burns.

4. How to Play: Select an Operator Card from your hand, then click a tile on the 4x4 grid to introduce species or restoration interventions!
